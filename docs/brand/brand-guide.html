Analytics you can trace back to the source.

Telumera is a self-hosted, privacy-first platform for understanding websites: who visits, how fast it is, what breaks, what changed.

01 — The mark

Probe

A T drawn in parallel lines, like signal traces on a board. Two outlines frame a single centre line: the probe. At the foot the lines step back and the probe runs furthest. That is the brand's whole idea: everything we show you can be followed down to where it was measured.

Grid256 units. Crossbar 216 × 80, stem 80 wide: the bar is exactly as tall as the stem is wide.
LinesFive lines, 10 units thick, on a 17.5 pitch. Gaps are slightly narrower than lines so the mark holds together as one shape.
ProbeThe centre line is the only one that runs into the crossbar's middle and the only one that can take colour. Use Signal on it, or nothing.
FootOuter lines stop at 188, inner at 206, probe at 224. Three equal steps: never change their order or spacing.
OpticsThe whole drawing sits 4 units above centre so it doesn't look like it's sinking.
02 — Small sizes

A separate cut for 16 to 32 px

Five lines can't survive at favicon size, so the small cut is drawn on a 16-pixel grid: the outer outline plus the probe, one pixel each, one pixel apart. It snaps to whole pixels at 16, 32 and 48 px.

03 — Lockups

Mark and name, fixed together

The name is set in Chakra Petch Regular, uppercase, letter-spaced +260 and converted to outlines. The cap height is half the mark's height. The gap between mark and name equals the crossbar's height.

Horizontal · default
Stacked · square spaces
Reversed · on Graphite
One colour · print, stamps, engraving

Clear space

Keep a free margin equal to the crossbar's height (the dashed box above) on all sides.

Minimum size

Horizontal lockup 120 px / 30 mm wide. Full mark 40 px. Below that, use the small cut.

Backgrounds

Bone, white or Graphite. On photos, place the mark on a solid Graphite or Bone plate.

04 — Colour

Graphite, Bone and one signal

The brand is almost monochrome. Signal orange is a marker, not a fill: the probe line, the primary chart series, focus rings, the active item. If a screen has more than a few touches of Signal, it has too many.

05 — Typography

Engineered for headings, plain for reading

Chakra Petch gives headings and big numbers their cut corners. Red Hat Text carries every interface label and paragraph. Red Hat Mono is for raw values: event IDs, tokens, query timings. All three are free (OFL) and allow logo use.

06 — Module icons

One icon per module, today and later

Every module on the roadmap gets an icon drawn the way the mark is drawn: straight lines, square corners, one stroke weight, and a single accent line that lights up in Signal when the module is active.

24-unit grid, 1.5 stroke. Miter joins, flat ends. No curves except where the object is round (gauge).

One accent per icon. The accent is the "probe": the line that carries the meaning. Grey when idle, Signal when active.

Planned modules are shown, not hidden. They appear in navigation as dimmed and labelled "Planned", so the platform's direction is visible.

07 — Don't

Keep the mark intact

The steps at the foot and the single coloured line are the mark. Most mistakes break one of those two.

Don't stretch or squash it
Don't recolour the probe
Don't rotate or flip it
Don't use the small cut above 48 px
